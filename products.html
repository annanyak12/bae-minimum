Our sample collection illustrates the BAE MINIMUM approach to minimalist skincare: familiar product types, simple descriptions and an easy-to-browse shop. Use the search and filters below to explore the mock catalogue by product name, category or price.

From a cleanser and daily moisturiser to a sunscreen concept, serum, face mist or lip balm, each listing is included to demonstrate how a skincare brand could organise an online product range. The names, prices and product details are fictional examples created for an academic project.

Please note: this is not a functioning retail store. Product descriptions are illustrative, not medical advice or verified formulation claims. No real products are sold, no orders are fulfilled, and the checkout is a demonstration only.
