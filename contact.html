BAE MINIMUM is based on the idea that self-care can be simple, accessible and personal. This website presents a sample skincare brand identity, an illustrative product catalogue and a few common shopping journeys, including a wishlist, account screens and demo checkout.

If you are reviewing the project, the frequently asked questions below explain which features are simulated and how the concept is intended to work. No real payments, orders, accounts or product deliveries are provided through this site.

Frequently asked questions

What is BAE MINIMUM?

A student skincare brand concept built on the idea that self-care is not selfish. BAE stands for “Before Anybody Else.”

Are these real products?

No. All products, prices and formulations are fictional demonstration data, and we make no clinical or medical claims.

Can I place a real order?

No. Checkout is a demo that generates a sample reference. No payment is taken and nothing is shipped.

What does a simple routine look like?

Morning: cleanse, moisturise, sunscreen. Evening: cleanse, moisturise. Add a serum or lip balm if you like. That is the whole idea.

Is the login real?

No. It is a browser-only simulation for demonstration. Please do not use a real password.
